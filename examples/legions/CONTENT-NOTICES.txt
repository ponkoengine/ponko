Legions: content notices
========================

Legions is a showcase game for the Ponko engine, built only from content
made for it. Nothing in it is taken from another work.

Pictures
--------
The game ships no picture files. Its soldiers, archers, arrows and the
fallen are drawn into a sprite sheet by the game's own code when it starts,
and the ground is painted from the map. Both are part of the program, which
is Copyright (c) 2026 Leon Mamić and Ponko contributors, licensed under the
MIT license or the Apache License 2.0, at your option (see LICENSE-MIT and
LICENSE-APACHE in this package).

Replays
-------
skirmish.json            a short recorded battle of 2,000 units, made by the
                         game's own recorder

Text and fonts
--------------
The game ships no font file. Its text is drawn with the default fonts bundled
into the engine by the `epaint_default_fonts` crate; their licenses are
included in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt together with every other library that is
linked into the game.
