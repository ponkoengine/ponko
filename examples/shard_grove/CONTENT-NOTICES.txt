Shard Grove: content notices
============================

Shard Grove is the 3D reference game for the Ponko engine, built only from
content made for it. Nothing in it is taken from another work.

Models (glTF 2.0, binary)
-------------------------
shard_grove/grove.glb        the walled grove: ground, walls, broken walls,
                             pillars, a rock, the platform and its ramp, the
                             gate's arch
shard_grove/warden.glb       the warden: a skinned character of 8 joints with
                             the clips idle, walk, pulse and hurt
shard_grove/crawler.glb      the crawler: a skinned character of 6 joints with
                             the clips idle, crawl and stunned

Sounds (WAV, mono, 22,050 Hz)
