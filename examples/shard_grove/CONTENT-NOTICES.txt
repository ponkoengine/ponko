Shard Grove: content notices
============================

Shard Grove is the 3D reference game for the Ponko engine. Its characters,
textures, sounds and music are made by the artists named below and dedicated
to the public domain under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal; the full text
of that dedication is in CC0-1.0.txt in this package. CC0 needs no credit,
and the credits are given here all the same.

The grove's geometry was made for this game and is Copyright (c) 2026 Leon
Mamić and Ponko contributors, licensed under the MIT license or the Apache
License 2.0, at your option (see LICENSE-MIT and LICENSE-APACHE in this
package).

Models (glTF 2.0, binary)
-------------------------
shard_grove/grove.glb
    The walled grove: ground, walls, broken walls, pillars, a rock, the
    platform and its ramp, the gate's arch. Geometry made for this game.
    Its textures (base color, normal and ambient occlusion / roughness /
    metalness maps) are from Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com, CC0:
      the ground   "Forest Ground 01" by Rob Tuytel
                   https://polyhaven.com/a/forrest_ground_01
      the walls    "Castle Wall Slates" by Rob Tuytel
                   https://polyhaven.com/a/castle_wall_slates
      the ruins    "Stone Brick Wall 001" by Dimitrios Savva (photography)
                   and Rico Cilliers (processing)
                   https://polyhaven.com/a/stone_brick_wall_001
      the rock     "Mossy Rock" by Rob Tuytel
                   https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_rock

shard_grove/warden.glb
    The warden: "Hooded Adventurer" by Quaternius, CC0,
    https://poly.pizza/m/y9KWOVG21R (https://quaternius.com). A skinned
    character with the clips idle, walk, pulse and hurt (named Idle, Walk,
    Interact and HitRecieve in the original).

shard_grove/crawler.glb
    The crawler: "Crab Enemy" by Quaternius, CC0,
    https://poly.pizza/m/Gs3yfsV5lB (https://quaternius.com). A skinned
    character with the clips idle, crawl and stunned (named Idle, Walk and
    HitRecieve in the original).
