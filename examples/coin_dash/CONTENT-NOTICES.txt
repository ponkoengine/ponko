Coin Dash: content notices
==========================

Coin Dash is a reference game for the Ponko engine. Its pictures, sounds and
music are made by the artists named below and dedicated to the public domain
under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal; the full text of that dedication is
in CC0-1.0.txt in this package. CC0 needs no credit, and the credits are given
here all the same. One font is under the SIL Open Font License (see "Text and
fonts").
