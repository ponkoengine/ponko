Coin Dash: content notices
==========================

Coin Dash is a reference game for the Ponko engine, built from content made
for it, and one font under the SIL Open Font License (see "Text and fonts").

Pictures
--------
assets/coin_dash/coin.png
    The coin: a 32 x 32 picture drawn by a short script for this example.
    Copyright (c) 2026 Leon Mamić and Ponko contributors.
    Licensed under the MIT license or the Apache License 2.0, at your option
    (see LICENSE-MIT and LICENSE-APACHE in this package).

The player's disc is drawn in code when the game starts; it is not a file.

Sound
-----
There are no sound files. Every sound effect is a sine tone that the game
synthesizes when it starts.

Text and fonts
--------------
Latin text is drawn with the default fonts bundled into the engine by the
`epaint_default_fonts` crate; their licenses are included in
THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt together with every other library that is linked into
the game.

assets/coin_dash/fonts/NotoSansArabic-UI.ttf
    The Arabic text: a subset of Noto Sans Arabic.
    Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors.
    Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, copied with the subset's
    description in assets/coin_dash/fonts/OFL.txt.

assets/coin_dash/lang/en.json, assets/coin_dash/lang/ar.json
    The game's text in English and Arabic, written for this example, under
    the same terms as the coin picture.
